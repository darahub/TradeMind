Instead of writing bots, managing infrastructure, or coding complex strategy logic, users describe what they want:

> “Buy BTC when ETF inflows exceed $500M and rebalance weekly.”

TradeMind transforms those ideas into structured strategy workflows powered by SoSoValue market intelligence, historical simulations, SoDEX execution, and eventually an on-chain strategy marketplace.

In simple terms:

> From one sentence to a monetizable autonomous trading strategy.

## Architecture

TradeMind is designed around five core layers.

---

### 1. Strategy Reasoning Layer

Users interact conversationally with an AI strategy engine.

The system converts plain-English instructions into:

* structured trading logic
* entry/exit conditions
* risk constraints
* rebalance schedules
* allocation rules
* execution workflows

This becomes the foundation of the autonomous strategy.

---

### 2. Market Intelligence Layer

This layer continuously ingests SoSoValue market intelligence.

The system monitors:

* ETF flows
* macro conditions
* narrative momentum
* sector strength
* volatility conditions
* historical strategy performance
* SSI rankings
* index trends

This is where TradeMind understands market behavior.

---

### 3. Backtesting & Simulation Layer

Before deployment, strategies are validated against historical market conditions.

The system evaluates:

* win rates
* drawdowns
* Sharpe ratios
* volatility exposure
* correlation behavior
* relative benchmark performance

This prevents users from deploying untested strategy logic blindly.

---

### 4. Risk Management Layer

Every strategy passes through automated safety validation.

Planned safeguards include:

* maximum drawdown protection
* position limits
* exposure controls
* confidence thresholds
* execution slippage checks
* kill switches
* rebalance frequency controls
* emergency strategy pause

The goal is controlled autonomous execution rather than reckless automated trading.

---

### 5. Execution & Marketplace Layer

Approved strategies become live autonomous agents connected to SoDEX.

Possible actions include:

* opening positions
* rotating portfolios
* rebalancing allocations
* executing macro-driven shifts
* compounding vault rewards
* updating strategy exposure

Strategies can later be listed publicly where users:

* allocate capital
* copy-trade strategies
* monitor performance
* pay performance fees
* follow top-performing agents

Example:

> “Entering AI sector exposure because ETF inflows increased, macro yields weakened, and AI narrative momentum strengthened over the past 7 days.”
